How the text maps onto the model

Competence coefficient Every voter has a credential score. When a voter updates, each neighbour's opinion counts with weight (0.05 + credential + earned stake) raised to the competence weighting power. At 0 it is one person, one vote; at 3 a PhD outweighs a crowd member about 40 to 1.

The PhD vs the gut feeling Each turn a voter may see private evidence. Credentialed voters read the truth well, but through the field's current paradigm. The crowd's evidence is barely better than chance. That is the econometrics versus the daytime TV.

A layered topology Credentialed voters get long-range links to each other. The layered topology slider strengthens those links and mutes what they hear from crowd neighbours, so expert opinion clumps into its own layer.

Valuable yet unusual opinions A few hidden mavericks have low credentials but sharp, current evidence. When the truth shifts (the fishery changes), the experts' paradigm lags with the inertia setting, and the mavericks are right first. With heavy stake weighting their neighbours drown them out.

Treat them as hallucinations, let some leak A crowd voter who dissents from the expert consensus and has evidence behind it may leak into the residual stream. It is flagged in yellow, gets a hearing inside the expert layer, and after 20 ticks faces a test that is right with the scrutiny accuracy probability.

Flagging for increased scrutiny Passing earns the voter lasting stake, and a passed true insight also speeds up the experts' paradigm shift. Failing costs stake. Weak scrutiny lets hallucinations gain stake too, so watch the bottom-right counter.

The five fishery explanations are illustrative labels. The model is a toy built to make the trade-offs visible, not a calibrated forecast.
