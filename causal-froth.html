Secret order

Live. Hidden sites flash a cue, then bloom after a fixed lag. Every agent can see the flash; only thinkers can learn what it means.

How the kinds differ. Drifters (scale 1) walk at random and eat what they bump into. Chasers (reactive baseline) head for the richest cell they can see right now: stimulus, then response. Their pace sets how many steps they take per tick: at zero they never move and their resting drain drops to 40%, so they live like filter feeders on whatever the flow brings; above one, each extra step costs more than the last. Modelers (scale 2) assume the froth has seams: they estimate its flow, head upstream when nothing is in view, treat rivals as part of the world, and learn what a cue predicts. Builders (scale 3) are modelers that also build seams, using their flow estimate to aim the mouth of their pocket. Scale 4 is the secret-order switch. Every agent pays a cost to live and to move; thinkers pay extra each tick for their model.
